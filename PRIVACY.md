# Privacy Policy

## Data Collection
Job Application Assistant does not collect any user data. Your resume data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API and is never transmitted to our servers.

## Data Transmission
Only the job posting content and open-ended question text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing. Your locally-stored resume data is never sent unless you explicitly fill a form.

## Personal Information
Your resume, including name, email, phone, and work history, remains entirely on your device and is never shared with third parties.
